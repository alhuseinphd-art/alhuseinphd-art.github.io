About

I am an English language instructor and applied linguist with more than 13 years of teaching experience in Sudan and Qatar. I hold a PhD in Applied Linguistics from Sudan University of Science and Technology. I focus on each learner's needs and on interactive, safe learning environments, and I help teachers and researchers work smarter with AI and digital research tools.


AI tools should support the researcher's judgement, not replace it.
