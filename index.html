About

I am an English language educator with a background in EFL and teacher education (PGCE). My work sits where language teaching, research methods and educational technology meet. I design hands-on workshops and practical, reusable resources that make research and AI tools approachable for everyone.


AI tools should support the researcher's judgement, not replace it.
